# Introduction

This project **Retail store Analyis** report is designed to analyze sales data and generate insights that help improve business performance.

It aims to:
- Understand sales trends (what sells, when, and how much).
- Evaluate product performance (best and worst-selling items).
- Analyze customer behavior (buying patterns, frequency).
- Track revenue and profitability.
- Support data-driven decisions like pricing, stocking, and promotions.

Overall, the goal is to turn raw retail data into useful insights that help the business increase sales, reduce losses, and operate more efficiently.

# Problem Statement
The retail store generates significant sales across different products, regions, and customer segments, but lacks clear visibility into what drives revenue and profit performance. There is a need to analyze sales patterns, customer behavior, product contribution, and regional performance to identify growth opportunities, improve profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.

# Key Posing Questions
- Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which category contributes most to overall sales?
- Who are the top customers and how much do they contribute?
- Which salesperson drives the most revenue?
- What region performs best in terms of revenue?
- How do sales and profit vary over time (monthly/yearly trends)?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What is the profit margin by shipping mode?
- Are there underperforming products or regions that need attention?
- How can the store increase revenue and optimize profit based on these insights?

# Dataset Overview

This dataset contains **1000 orders** it captures detailed informations which includes the following:

- **Order ID** - It represents unique identifier for each transaction.
- **Order Date** - It represents date when the order was placed.
- **Customer ID** - It represents unique ID assigned to each customer.
- **Customer Name** - It represents name of the customer who made the purchase.
- **Address** - It represents customer’s street address.
- **City** - It represents city where the customer is located. 
- **State** - It represents state or region of the customer.
- **Sales Person** - It represents staff responsible for the sale.
- **Region** - It represents sales region (e.g., West, East, South, Midwest).
- **Country** - It represents country where the customer resides.
- **Shipped Date** - It represents date the order was shipped.
- **Shippers Name** - It represents the logistics company responsible for transporting and delivering the package.
- **Ship Name** - It represents the shipping service type used for the shipment.
- **Ship Address** - It represents delivery address.
- **Ship City** - It represents city of delivery.
- **Ship State** - It represents state of delivery. 
- **Payment Type** - It represents method used for payment (e.g. Cash, Card, PayPal).
- **Product Name** - It represents name of the product purchased.
- **Category** - It represents product category (e.g. Electronics, Accessories).
- **Unit Price** - It represents price of a single unit of the product.
- **Quantity** - It represents number of units purchased.
- **Revenue** - It represents total sales amount (Unit Price × Quantity).
- **Shipping Fee** - It represents cost charged for delivering the order.

# Measures (DAX)
Created calculated measures using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and evaluate business performance across customer, product, sales, and delivery dimensions. These measures served as the foundation of the dashboard, enabling dynamic analysis of revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and operational efficiency across different time periods.

*The DAX measures developed included:*

- **Total Revenue** – Calculated total sales generated across all transactions.
- **Total Profit** – Measured overall profit earned after deducting product and shipping costs.
- **Profit Margin (%)** – Evaluated profitability as a percentage of revenue.
- **Average Order Value** – Measured the average amount spent per order.
- **Customer Lifetime Revenue** – Calculated the total revenue generated by each customer throughout their relationship with the business.
- **Purchase Frequency** – Measured how often customers placed orders.
- **Order Count** – Counted the total number of orders placed.
- **Sales Volume per Product** – Measured total units sold for each product.
- **Revenue per Product** – Calculated revenue contribution by individual products.
- **Product Profit** – Determined profit generated by each product.
- **Product Profit Margin (%)** – Evaluated profitability at the product level.
- **On-Time Delivery Rate (%)** – Measured the percentage of orders delivered within the expected timeframe.
- **Average Delivery Time** – Calculated the average number of days taken to deliver orders.
- **Shipping Cost Percentage (%)** – Assessed the proportion of shipping costs relative to revenue.
- **Shipping Efficiency Score** – Evaluated delivery performance against shipping costs and timelines.
  
**Purpose:** These DAX measures enabled interactive reporting and trend analysis across daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly periods, providing stakeholders with actionable insights into sales performance, customer retention, product profitability, and delivery efficiency.

# Key Metrics (Top of the Page): Revenue, Profit, Total Orders, Top Region, Top Category.#

- **Revenue:** The total amount of sales generated from all products during the selected period.
- **Profit:** The total earnings remaining after deducting all associated costs from revenue.
- **Total Orders:** The total number of orders placed by customers, indicating the volume of sales transactions.
- **Top Region:** The region that generated the highest revenue, highlighting the company's strongest performing market.
- **Top Category:** The product category that contributed the highest revenue, indicating the most successful product segment.
  
These KPIs provide a high-level overview of business performance by summarizing sales activity, profitability, customer demand, and market performance. They help stakeholders quickly identify key revenue drivers, monitor operational success, and make informed business decisions.

# Visuals

- **Product vs Revenue (Column Chart):** This visual compares revenue generated by different products. It helps identify the best-performing and lowest-performing products, enabling better inventory planning and product management decisions.
- **Salesperson vs Revenue (Bar Chart):** This visual measures the revenue contribution of each salesperson. It supports performance evaluation by identifying top-performing sales representatives and opportunities for improvement.
- **Category vs Revenue (Pie Chart):** This chart shows the percentage contribution of each product category to total revenue. It helps stakeholders understand which categories drive the largest share of sales.
- **Customer Name vs Revenue (Bar Chart):** This visual highlights revenue generated by individual customers. It helps identify high-value customers and supports customer retention strategies.
- **Region vs Revenue (Pie Chart):** This chart illustrates how revenue is distributed across different regions. It helps management identify strong-performing markets and areas requiring additional business focus.
- **Payment Type vs Revenue (Column Chart):** This visual analyzes revenue generated through various payment methods. It provides insight into customer payment preferences and transaction patterns.
